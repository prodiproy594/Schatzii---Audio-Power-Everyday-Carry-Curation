Field Notes: The Wireless Audio Brief

01 • Ergonomics & In-Ear Grip

Why Ultra-Small Earbuds Excel on the Move

Bulky heavy earbuds continually suffer from downward momentum, slipping out when running or commuting. At only 4.7 grams, the Bullet 2.0 sits deep and flush inside your ear canal, creating a tight acoustic seal that blocks ambient noise naturally without ear fatigue.

02 • Emergency Phone Power Strategy

The Power-Audio Matrix: Ditch the Extra Battery Brick

Carrying heavy power bricks and tangle-prone cables adds unnecessary drag to your daily carry. The Bullet 2.0's custom aerospace capsule fulfills both needs: keeping your wireless music charged for weeks, or supplying emergency charging to your phone when you are stranded away from a wall outlet.
