Field Notes: The Wireless Audio Brief

01 • Ergonomics & Passive Isolation

Why Ultra-Small Earbuds Sound Better on the Move

Traditional heavy earbuds rely on ear hooks or silicone wings because their bulk causes downward drag during movement. Because the Bullet 2.0 weighs almost nothing, it sits deep and flush in your ear canal, creating an acoustic seal that naturally blocks external noise without battery-draining active filters.

02 • Travel Carry Strategy

The Power-Audio Matrix: Ditch the Extra Battery Pack

Carrying dedicated phone power banks and separate earbud chargers adds unnecessary clutter to your EDC pouch. The Bullet 2.0’s 2100mAh aerospace capsule combines both: keep your audio fully fueled for weeks, or plug your phone into the capsule when stranded with a drained battery.
